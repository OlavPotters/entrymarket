Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This notice explains how we handle the personal data you share with us through entrymarket.com. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Entry is a trade name of Hubstance Netherlands B.V., registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 90978293. We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at hello@entrymarket.com.

What we collect and why

  • Waitlist. Your email address, so we can tell you when Entry launches. We process it on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Investor requests. Your name, email address, company and role, so we can respond to your request and share information about Entry. We process these details to take steps at your request and on the basis of our legitimate interest in engaging with prospective investors.

We do not sell your data, use it for profiling or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies or tracking. Your browser stores a single setting for the duration of your visit so the opening animation is not repeated. When you load the site, your browser connects to Google Fonts to load the typeface, which means your IP address is shared with Google.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run this website and handle your messages: GitHub (website hosting), FormSubmit (delivering form submissions to our inbox) and Microsoft (email). Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards available under the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Waitlist details are kept until you unsubscribe or until 12 months after launch, whichever comes first. Investor requests are kept for up to two years after our last contact, unless we enter into a further relationship that requires longer retention.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent. Send your request to hello@entrymarket.com and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
